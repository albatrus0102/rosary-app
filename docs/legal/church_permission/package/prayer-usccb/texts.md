# Rosarium — texts I intend to use (United States)

These are the texts the app would show, as text only and unchanged. Each one is given with its source. Please confirm them, or correct them: I will use exactly the wording you approve, with the credit line you ask for. Where a text is marked 〔확인 필요〕 (“to be confirmed”), I could not verify it and I ask you to provide or confirm it. Line breaks and spelling are copied as published, including any evident misprints.

**A note on verification.** I have not been able to consult the texts published by the USCCB itself (“Prayers of the Rosary”, “How to Pray the Rosary”, the Rosary booklet). Every text below therefore comes from the Compendium on vatican.va, except the Fatima prayer. If the form you ask Catholics in the United States to use differs anywhere, please tell me and I will follow yours.

### The Sign of the Cross

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

```text
In the name of the Father
and of the Son
and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
```

### The Apostles’ Creed

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

This is the wording printed in the Compendium, not the translation of The Roman Missal (© 2010 ICEL). 〔확인 필요〕 Which of the two do you ask people to use when praying the Rosary?

```text
I believe in God the Father almighty,
Creator of heaven and earth.
And in Jesus Christ, His only Son,
our Lord, Who was conceived by the Holy Spirit,
born of the Virgin Mary,
suffered under Pontius Pilate,
was crucified, died, and was buried.
He descended into hell; the third day
He rose again from the dead;
He ascended into heaven, and sits at
the right hand of God the Father
almighty, from thence He shall come
to judge the living and the dead.
I believe in the Holy Spirit,
the holy Catholic Church,
the communion of saints,
the forgiveness of sins,
the resurrection of the body
and life everlasting.
Amen.
```

### The Our Father

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

```text
Our Father who art in heaven,
hallowed be thy name.
Thy kingdom come.
Thy will be done
on earth, as it is in heaven.
Give us this day
our daily bread,
and forgive us our trespasses,
as we forgive those who trespass against us,
and lead us not into temptation,
but deliver us from evil.
```

### The Hail Mary

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

```text
Hail, Mary, full of grace,
the Lord is with thee.
Blessed art thou among women
and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus.
Holy Mary, Mother of God,
pray for us sinners,
now and at the hour of our death.
Amen.
```

### The Glory Be

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

```text
Glory be to the Father
and to the Son
and to the Holy Spirit,
as it was in the beginning
is now, and ever shall be
world without end. Amen.
```

### The Fatima prayer

Source: the form commonly printed in Rosary leaflets in the United States, as recorded in my own research notes. **Status: 〔확인 필요〕 not verified against any official publication.** Please confirm or correct it.

```text
O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell; lead all souls to heaven, especially those in most need of thy mercy.
```

### Hail, Holy Queen

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

The Compendium prints a “UK version” and a “USA version”; this is the **USA version**.

```text
Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of Mercy,
our life, our sweetness and our hope.
To you do we cry,
poor banished children of Eve.
To you do we send up our sighs,
mourning and weeping in this valley of tears
Turn then, most gracious advocate,
your eyes of mercy toward us,
and after this exile
show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb,
Jesus.
O clement, O loving,
O sweet Virgin Mary.
```

### Versicle and concluding prayer

Source: copied word for word on 23 November 2026 from the Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church, English edition (© 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana), https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html

```text
V. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God.
R. That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.
Let us pray.
O God, whose only-begotten Son,
by his life, death and resurrection,
has purchased for us
the rewards of eternal life,
grant, we beseech thee,
that meditating on these mysteries
of the most holy Rosary of the
Blessed Virgin Mary,
we may imitate what they contain
and obtain what they promise,
through the same Christ our Lord.
Amen.
```
