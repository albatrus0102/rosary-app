# Rosarium — English

**What it is.** “Rosarium” is a free Android app that helps people pray the Rosary and the Stations of the Cross, made by a Korean lay Catholic as an individual.

**Who makes it, and why.** One person, not a company: a long-time Korean Catholic whose whole family is Catholic (their child, baptised as an infant, served at the altar). The app began so that the family could pray the Rosary as they did in the parish they once attended, and to let people who may never visit them pray, at least indirectly, in famous churches. Other apps of this kind have irreverent elements (avatars walking onto the altar) or incorrect prayers; this one is meant to be proper and reverent. No revenue at all: the author bears every cost. The hope: more occasions for Catholics to pray the Rosary, and a more approachable Church for those who are not Catholic.

**Free and non-commercial.** No advertising, no paid features, no donations. It is not an official service of the Church and says so.

**Why we ask you.** I understand that the texts on your website and in your publications are your copyright, and that your Guidelines for the Publication of Liturgical Books (https://www.usccb.org/committees/divine-worship/policies/guidelines-for-the-publication-of-liturgical-books) ask for Church approval of prayer books. I could not open your web pages from where I am (they returned an error), so I have not been able to read the official texts or your permissions policy myself. That is why I ask you for the texts as well as for the permission. This is my understanding; please correct me if a different procedure applies.

**What we ask.** Permission (or confirmation that none is needed) to show the prayer texts below, unchanged, with your credit line.

a) that you send me, or point me to, the authoritative text of each prayer below in the form approved for use in the United States, so that I do not copy an unverified version from elsewhere;
b) the prayers are: the Sign of the Cross, the Apostles’ Creed, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Glory Be, the Fatima prayer (“O my Jesus …”), the Hail Holy Queen with its versicle, and the closing prayer (“O God, whose only-begotten Son …”), together with the names of the twenty mysteries — which form of each you ask Catholics in the United States to use, and, if you can say, where the texts used in the United Kingdom and Ireland differ;
c) whether I may show these texts in a free app, and with what credit line. I understand that you may not currently grant permission for posting texts on the internet; if that applies to an app, please tell me, and I will not use your text. If the copyright in any of them belongs to ICEL or to someone else, please tell me whom to ask;
d) whether an app of this kind needs an imprimatur or other Church approval under your Guidelines for the Publication of Liturgical Books, and whom I should ask;

**How the texts are used.**

- Shown as text on the screen, unchanged, with the credit line you specify, on the prayer screen and on a “Prayer sources” page.
- Text only. English prayers are never read aloud by the app: no recorded voice, no synthetic (AI) voice, no text-to-speech. (Only the Korean edition has a voice, under a separate request to the Korean bishops’ conference.)
- For praying in two parts, each prayer is shown as a leader’s part and a response; nothing is altered, and the two parts together are the complete text. An optional “short view” shows only the opening words of prayers the user knows by heart.
- The app contains no Scripture text (references only), no Mass texts and no hymns.
- A free app on Google Play, available worldwide.

**Without an answer.** If I receive no answer, I will not copy any text from your website or publications. The English (US) edition will use only traditional texts that are clearly in the public domain, with their source shown. If you grant permission, the app will state the scope of that permission inside the app, and I will not add anything beyond it that could cause misunderstanding. If you wish, I will gladly ask persons you designate to review the app.
