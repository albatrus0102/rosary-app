# Online / social prayer apps: survey for a Korean Catholic rosary app with a shared "cyber cathedral"

Researched 2026-11-12 from public web sources (help centres, app-store listings, press, privacy audits). No apps were installed or used hands-on, so navigation details come from vendor documentation and listings and may lag the current builds.

**Confidence legend**
- **[V]** verified against a source fetched in this survey (URL cited)
- **[S]** from a search-result summary of the cited source, not read in full
- **[U]** unverified: I could not find a usable source; stated from general background knowledge and should be checked before relying on it

**Not covered / gaps.** Sources for 마리아사랑넷, 카카오 오픈채팅 기도방, Moravian Daily Texts, "Pray with Me", and cpbc live-chat behaviour could not be found or fetched; they appear below only as [U] notes. ## 1. Service-by-service notes

### 1.1 Hallow (Catholic, global market leader)
- **What:** audio-guided Catholic prayer and meditation (rosary, Lectio, examen, sleep, music), freemium with a subscription of about $70/yr [S].
- **Navigation [V]:** five bottom tabs: **Home / Discover / Bible / Church (community) / Me**. Home = daily quote, current community challenge, campaigns, suggested prayers and routines. Me = streak (only if switched on), routines, recently prayed, favourites, downloads, intentions, reflections; settings behind a gear icon. The community tab is absent where a parish has not launched or the country is unsupported.
- **Praying together [V]:** asynchronous, not live rooms.
  - *Intentions:* posted to friends, a group or the parish; private or shared; shown in a **story-style carousel** at the top of the tab plus an activity feed; **expire after 7 days**; people respond by chat or by tapping "Pray for an Intention" when starting a prayer, so the intention is attached to a real prayer session.
  - *Groups:* custom groups are **not public or searchable**, joined only by direct invite link; only church communities are searchable. A group is created with a name, a purpose and a start date for praying a collection together.
  - *Challenges:* Lent/Advent "Pray40"-style community challenges, plus church-specific ones.
  - *Presence:* a simple count of how many others are praying with you [S].
- **Moderation [V]:** report intentions, user profiles and community profiles (reason picker, optional details) to a central moderation team that can remove content or ban; **block** (non-friends: stops messages and friend requests, hides their intentions/posts) and **mute** (friends). Parish admins get a dashboard with a **Reported** tab. Group admins can remove members, require join approval (paid partner plans), and **turn off member messaging so only admins post**.
- **Privacy controls [V]:** per-group toggles for whether your intentions, reflection answers and **prayer completions** are shared with the group and friends; last choice is remembered.
- **Notifications [V]:** categories for new prayers, campaigns, daily quotes; routines for scheduled prayer.
- **Identity [V]:** editable name and avatar; email or phone login; account deletion in settings.
- **Works:** content depth, polish, challenges as seasonal on-ramps, opt-in streak.
- **Complaints:** "pay-to-pray" pricing and celebrity-driven marketing [S]; Trustpilot aggregate cited as 2.5/5 from 25 reviews (small sample) [S]; privacy policy permits ad-targeting data sharing although the company says it has not done so [V]; removed from the China App Store (2024) and withdrawn from the EU (2025), which the CEO attributed to regulation of religious-data apps [S].
- Sources: https://help.hallow.com/en/articles/9650908-overview-of-the-mobile-app · https://help.hallow.com/en/articles/3949390-overview-of-hallow-groups · https://help.hallow.com/en/articles/10659081-faq-for-group-admins-in-the-church-tab · https://intercom.help/hallow-inc/en/articles/10619403-how-do-i-report-inappropriate-behavior-in-the-app · https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/hallow-app-controversy-why-some-users-call-it-money-grab-why-its-banned-some-countries-1738240 · https://toolradar.com/tools/hallow · https://www.catholicculture.org/commentary/hallow-prayer-and-meditation-app-all-that-and-more/

### 1.2 Pray.com (evangelical, social network + audio)
- **What:** Christian social network with audio Bible stories, sleep content and church groups; freemium.
- **Praying together [V/S]:** public, Facebook-like feed inside large "prayer communities" (some near 100,000 members) where people post requests and others respond; churches run closed groups with discussion, livestreams and giving, and Pray.com takes a cut of donations.
- **Privacy and safety (the cautionary tale) [V]:**
  - Logged location, clicked links and the **text of posts**; enriched profiles with data-broker data (religion, marital status, income); sent content-consumption data to Facebook and attribution vendors, two of which were not disclosed.
  - A **2020 breach** exposed home addresses, church locations and contact lists via open cloud storage, including photos of minors.
  - A profile presenting as a **12-year-old posted about suicidal thoughts** in a ~100k-member group and was removed only after a journalist sent screenshots.
  - Users had shared a child's death, a spouse's suicide, addiction, infidelity and eviction, and said they assumed privacy: "there is an expectation of privacy among Christians sharing prayers."
- **Complaints [S]:** unexpected $49.99-$59 charges after trials; Trustpilot 1.5/5 from 91 reviews; Mozilla *Privacy Not Included* warning.
- Sources: https://www.yahoo.com/news/nothing-sacred-apps-sell-prayers-153708345.html · https://www.npr.org/2022/02/10/1079944694/what-we-can-learn-about-privacy-from-faith-based-apps · https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pray.com?page=3 · https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/privacynotincluded/praycom/ · https://www.scross.co.za/2018/05/pray-com-a-community-for-praying/

### 1.3 YouVersion Bible App: Prayer
- **What:** prayer list inside the largest Bible app; free, no ads.
- **Praying together [V]:**
  - Create a prayer (title + details), keep it private or **share with in-app friends only**; the author chooses whether recipients may re-share it (a controlled chain).
  - **"Pray" button registers once per day** per prayer; the author is **notified when a friend prays**, and vice versa.
  - Author can post **updates** and mark **Answered**, which moves it to an Answered list; only the author edits.
  - **Sentence starters** ("I'm thankful for...", "I'm stressed about...") for people who do not know what to write; guided prayer and reminders.
- **Scale [S]:** one million prayers created in the first week; prayer-feature engagement up 46% in 2024.
- **Weak points [V]:** useless without friends on the app; account required.
- Sources: https://harp-carrot-622t.squarespace.com/blog/review-of-the-prayer-option-in-the-bibleapp · https://www.christiantelegraph.com/?p=4898 · https://my.bible.com/prayer

### 1.4 Click To Pray (Pope's Worldwide Prayer Network, official Vatican app)
- **What [V]:** free; three short prayer moments a day (morning offering, midday, night) tied to the Pope's monthly intention; seven languages; daily prayers also by email.
- **Praying together [V/S]:** "pray with the network" wall where registered users post intentions and others **click to pray**; a counter shows how many prayed; the Pope has a profile. Version 2.0 (2021) added a personal prayer planner with timed notifications and tips.
- **Safety [V]:** rated 13+, flagged as user-generated content, with age assurance and parental controls; privacy label "no data collected."
- **Complaints [V/S]:** iOS 3.7/5 (138 ratings); buggy, "feels like a beta," repeated sign-in and registration failures, prayers not persisting; requests for font-size and language settings.
- **Praise [V]:** "amazing to see Catholics across the world praying with one another"; having others pray for a posted intention "felt great."
- Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/click-to-pray/id934935942 · https://vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2021-10/click-to-pray-pope-prayer-intention-app-synod.html · https://popesprayerusa.net/2018/05/22/what-is-click-to-pray · https://aleteia.org/2021/10/22/try-this-new-and-improved-pope-approved-click-to-pray-app/

### 1.5 Echo Prayer (prayer-list manager with groups)
- **What [V]:** free core + Echo Plus subscription; iOS 4.8/5 (~22k ratings).
- **Features [V]:** prayer list with tags and Answered; share a prayer privately with a person or a group (invite links); **reminders by push or email** per prayer or group; **Pray Now** focus mode with optional timer; **"I prayed for you" notification with an optional short message**; ministry **feeds** to follow (replaces email prayer chains).
- **Complaints [V]:** thin onboarding and hidden buttons; too many taps; marking "answered" removes an ongoing prayer when users want to log several answers; **group board does not show who posted each request**; early notification glitches.
- **Privacy label [V]:** email, name, user content and user ID linked to identity; name used for marketing.
- Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/echo-prayer/id880759027 · https://biblequestions.info/reviews/review-echo-prayer-app/

### 1.6 Glorify (Christian daily devotional)
- **What [S]:** daily devotional flow (passage, reflection, prayer), streaks, reminders, groups for prayer requests and community annotations on passages; iOS 4.9/5 (~90k).
- **Complaints [S]:** **streak loss** ("lost my over 250 day streak... no way to restore it"), streak repair moved behind an update/paywall, and former free content now requiring a subscription. Privacy policy allows ad-targeting data sharing; company says it has not done it [V].
- Sources: https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/glorify-devotional-prayer/1490587079?country=kr · https://faith.tools/app/48-glorify · https://www.yahoo.com/news/nothing-sacred-apps-sell-prayers-153708345.html

### 1.7 Abide (Christian meditation and sleep)
- **What [S]:** solo audio meditations and sleep stories, $39.99/yr after a 7-day trial; **no real community feature found**.
- **Complaints [S]:** free tier too thin to be useful; subscription/crash bugs; Mozilla 2022 privacy flag.
- **Relevance:** a reference point for solo audio and paywall backlash, not for social prayer.

### 1.8 Pray As You Go and Sacred Space (Jesuit)
- **Pray As You Go [S]:** free, donation-funded daily audio prayer under 15 minutes (music, scripture, reflection questions), plus retreats and seasonal series; 20 years old in 2026, community of over half a million. No social layer; the "together" is one shared daily session for everyone.
- **Sacred Space [S]:** daily guided text prayer on the web and in an app; submitted intentions are **remembered at Mass and forwarded to Cistercian and Carmelite monasteries**, which is a strong trust signal.
- Sources: https://www.jesuit.org.uk/news/jesuit-prayer-app-pray-as-you-go-turns-20-serving-a-global-community-of-over-half-a-million · https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pray-as-you-go-daily-prayer/id865934048 · https://sacredspace.com/about/ · https://sacredspace.com/frequently-asked-questions/

### 1.9 Hozana (Catholic prayer communities and novenas)
- **What [V/S]:** free network of 1,000+ "communities" run by laypeople, parishes, dioceses and religious orders; over 25 million prayers claimed.
- **Mechanics [V/S]:** join a community (novena, retreat, daily Gospel, Lectio); its daily "publication" lands in your personal **prayer space** or by email; **novenas can start any day**, not on a fixed date; pick the days that suit you for a personal plan; intentions section and in-community chat; anyone can create a community.
- Sources: https://frontend-admin.hozana.org/en/page/introduction · https://hozana.org/en/communities · https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1477461388 · https://hozana.org/en/prayer/novena

### 1.10 Living Rosary apps: Rosario, GlobalRosary, RosaryOn, Rosary73
- **Rosario [V]:** Pauline Jaricot's Living Rosary. A **group of five** each prays **one decade (about 5 minutes) a day**, so the group completes a full rosary daily around a shared intention; each member receives an assigned mystery with a meditation. 350,000+ users claimed [S].
- **GlobalRosary [S]:** choose an intention, receive your mystery, pray one decade daily; reminders, a world map of the Living Rosary, 7 languages, free.
- **RosaryOn [S]:** platform for prayer groups aiming at round-the-clock continuous rosary.
- **Rosary73 [S]:** each participant records one prayer; the recordings are stitched into an audio rosary.
- Sources: https://rosario.app/ · https://fides.org/en/news/72224-EUROPE_FRANCE_Pauline_Jaricot_s_Living_Rosary_on_an_App · https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rosaryon/id1445592430

### 1.11 24-7 Prayer rooms / prayer-chain sign-ups
- **What [S]:** register a prayer room and get a free shared calendar; people **book one-hour slots**, optionally recurring weekly, so an intention is covered continuously; rooms may be physical or virtual. Companion app "Inner Room" turns the phone into a personal prayer room.

### 1.12 Live broadcast prayer: Taizé, Relevant Radio, livestream Mass
- **Taizé [S]:** evening prayer streamed live daily at 20:30 from the Church of Reconciliation, with replays of the two most recent prayers. One fixed daily appointment, no chat needed.
- **Relevant Radio [S]:** nightly live "Family Rosary Across America"; listeners phone in intentions that the host reads out.
- **Livestream Mass/adoration [U]:** YouTube streams with live chat are the usual vehicle; chat tends to fill with "Amen" and intention lines.

### 1.13 Church prayer-wall modules (Tithe.ly, Nucleus, Upper Room)
- **Pattern [S]:** optional **pre-moderation** (an admin approves each request before it is public, to stop spam and third-party personal details); staff remove posts with links, ads or spam; published community norms; Tithe.ly's wall sends **no push notifications**, so requests go unseen unless people open the wall.

### 1.14 Amen (Augustine Institute)
- **What [S]:** fully free Catholic app: daily Mass readings, 1,000+ sessions, rosary in standard / meditative / scriptural formats with optional music, personalised Today screen. No social layer. Shows that a free, donor-funded Catholic alternative exists.

**Finding that matters most:** none of the surveyed apps offers a networked, real-time shared rosary room. ## 3. (b) Top 10 patterns worth borrowing

1. **Attach the intention to the act of praying.** Hallow's "Pray for an Intention" at session start and Echo's Pray Now turn a tap into an actual prayer. For a rosary: pick or accept an intention before the first bead and offer the decade for it.
2. **"기도했습니다" tap with a quiet notification back.** YouVersion's once-a-day Pray button and Echo's "I prayed for you" with an optional short message are the most-praised social mechanics. Limit to one tap per person per day per intention so the count means people, not clicks.
3. **Intentions that expire.** Hallow's 7-day lifetime keeps the wall fresh and limits how long sensitive text stays exposed. Offer renew, plus "응답받았습니다 / 감사" closing (YouVersion Answered, Echo, 바오로딸's graces-received history).
4. **Living Rosary relay (고리기도).** Five people, one decade each, a full rosary every day (Rosario, GlobalRosary). Low daily cost, strong mutual accountability, and it maps directly onto a practice Korean Catholics already know.
5. **Presence as a number, not a chat.** Hallow's "N praying with you" and Click To Pray's counter give communion without conversation. In a cathedral metaphor: lit candles or filled pews.
6. **Fixed daily appointments.** Taizé at 20:30 and the nightly radio rosary show that one predictable time creates live community with no matchmaking. Schedule rosary hours (e.g. after the evening Angelus) instead of always-open empty rooms.
7. **Slot sign-up for continuous prayer.** 24-7 Prayer's hourly calendar suits 9일기도/54일기도 or a vigil for a specific intention.
8. **Invite-only small groups, searchable official communities.** Hallow's split (custom groups by link only, parishes searchable) keeps intimacy and gives 본당/레지오/구역 groups a home.
9. **Seasonal challenges as on-ramps.** Hallow's Lent/Advent challenges and Hozana's start-any-day novenas. For Korea: 5월 성모성월, 10월 묵주기도 성월, 사순/대림, 54일 기도.
10. **Prompts and a trusted destination for intentions.** YouVersion's sentence starters lower the barrier to writing; Sacred Space forwarding intentions to Mass and monasteries tells people their request goes somewhere real. Having intentions remembered at an actual Mass or by a religious community would be a strong differentiator.

Also worth keeping from Korean apps: per-prayer alarms by weekday (가톨릭), per-intention alarms and a 54일 calendar that selects the day's mystery (바오로딸), recorded voices for a "praying with others" feel, and cross-device backup.

## 4. (c) Safety, moderation and privacy patterns

**Design so that little needs moderating**
- Prefer structured interaction (tap, candle, count, preset responses such as "함께 기도합니다") over free-text replies. Hallow lets admins switch a group to admin-only posting.
- In live rooms, no open chat by default; the prayer text is the shared content.
- Expire intentions automatically (Hallow: 7 days).

**Moderation toolkit seen in the field**
- Report on intentions, profiles and groups with a reason picker; central review; removal and bans (Hallow).
- Block and mute at the user level, hiding the other person's content everywhere (Hallow).
- Group-level admins: remove members, approve joins, restrict posting; an organisation-level "Reported" queue (Hallow parish dashboard).
- Optional pre-moderation of public requests; removal of links, ads and spam; published norms (church prayer-wall tools).
- Age rating and parental controls where user content exists (Click To Pray is 13+).

**Privacy patterns**
- Visibility chosen per intention: private / group / everyone, with the last choice remembered (Hallow, YouVersion).
- Separate toggle for sharing **prayer completions**; activity should not be broadcast by default (Hallow).
- Re-share permission controlled by the author (YouVersion).
- Nickname or 세례명 rather than legal name for public spaces; KakaoTalk open chat's separate 오픈프로필 is the local precedent. Real-name verification (하상: 교적 + carrier) fits parish-bound features only and excludes overseas Koreans, catechumens and under-14s.
- Collect little. Several Catholic apps declare "no data collected" (Click To Pray, 가톨릭, 바오로딸). Account deletion in settings (Hallow).
- Treat prayer text as sensitive data: Korean law (개인정보 보호법) classes religious belief and health information as 민감정보 requiring separate consent, and intentions routinely contain both, often about third parties. This is my reading of the law, not legal advice; get it reviewed.
- No ad SDKs or attribution trackers on prayer content, and no data-broker enrichment (the Pray.com failures).

**Crisis handling**
- Intentions will include illness, bereavement, debt and suicidal thoughts (documented on Pray.com, including from a minor). Plan keyword detection that surfaces crisis resources (자살예방 상담전화 109, 생명의전화) and a fast human review path, and write guidance on what users should not post about other people.

## 5. (d) Pitfalls and negative reviews

- **Public feed at scale.** Pray.com users posted their worst moments to ~100,000 strangers while assuming privacy; the gap between perceived and actual audience is the core failure. Show the audience size before posting.
- **Monetising prayer.** "Pay-to-pray" backlash against Hallow; Glorify and Abide reviews turning negative when free features moved behind a subscription; surprise trial charges on Pray.com.
- **Data practices become the story.** Mozilla flagged 28 of 32 mental-health and prayer apps; regulators and app stores are part of the risk (Hallow's EU and China exits).
- **Streak anxiety.** Glorify users furious at losing 250-day streaks; 굿뉴스 묵주기도 had a day-count reset bug. Hallow makes the streak opt-in. For a 54일 기도, a lost count is a pastoral problem, not just a UX one: allow make-up days and never reset silently.
- **Reliability and login friction.** Click To Pray ("feels like a beta," repeated sign-ins, prayers not saved) and 하상 (bugs, completion not registering). Community features fail first when sync is flaky.
- **Readability regressions.** Korean rosary users are often older; a redesign that shrank text drew requests to revert. Font-size control was requested on Click To Pray too.
- **Empty rooms and friend-graph dependence.** YouVersion Prayer is hollow without friends; a prayer wall with no notifications goes unread (Tithe.ly). Seed with scheduled sessions and an always-populated general intention.
- **Thin onboarding.** Echo reviewers could not find features; too many taps to do the main thing.
- **Missing attribution or context.** Echo's group board not showing who posted; "answered" removing a prayer that is still ongoing.
- **Under-age users and identity.** Pray.com's under-16 rule was not enforced; 하상 excludes under-14s entirely. Decide the policy deliberately.
- **Spiritual critique.** Catholic commentators call app prayer "shallow" and gamified. Counters and badges should stay modest and never rank people.

## 6. (e) Opportunities for a Korean Catholic rosary app with a cyber cathedral

1. **Real-time shared rosary is an open niche.** No surveyed app has networked live rooms; Korean apps have no social layer at all. Synchronised beads, 선창/후창 alternation (the role model 굿뉴스 묵주기도 already uses offline), and a visible congregation would be distinctive.
2. **Digitise practices that already exist:** 고리기도 (Living Rosary relay), 54일 기도 done as a group, 레지오/구역/본당 groups, 연도 and prayers for the sick and the dead. These currently run on KakaoTalk rooms [U] with manual check-ins.
3. **Calm togetherness.** Presence through candles, pews and counts rather than feeds and comments; this is both safer and closer to how a church feels.
4. **Trust positioning.** Free core, no ads, minimal data, nickname/세례명 identity, clear audience labels. This answers the main complaints about the Western leaders.
5. **Offline anchor.** Have intentions offered at a real Mass or by a religious community (Sacred Space model); link scheduled rosaries to liturgical seasons and cpbc-style fixed hours.
6. **Accessibility for older users:** large text setting, simple flows, audio-led prayer, reliable alarms (삼종기도 and per-intention), device backup.
7. **Diaspora:** overseas Korean Catholics are shut out of 교적-verified apps and asked the 가톨릭 app for overseas dioceses; time-zone-friendly scheduled rooms would serve them.
